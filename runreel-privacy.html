1. Scope

RunReel records running, walking, treadmill, trail running, hiking, and cycling activities and helps users create route stories, share images, and videos. RunReel is a general fitness app, not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease. BMI, calories, GPS, elevation, pace, and similar values may be estimates.

2. Information RunReel processes

  • Account: email address, Firebase user ID, authentication and email-verification state. RunReel does not store plaintext passwords.
  • Location: precise GPS position, timestamp, accuracy, speed, bearing, and altitude for outdoor route recording.
  • Activity and fitness: activity type, distance, duration, moving/rest time, pace or speed, steps, cadence, laps, elevation, highest point, and estimated calories.
  • Health profile and goals: height, weight history, informational BMI, unit preferences, and weekly or monthly distance goals.
  • User content: Route Story titles and descriptions, and photos or videos explicitly selected with Android Photo Picker.
  • Technical information: consent state and service/device request data processed by Firebase, Firebase App Check, Mapbox, Google Play services, and Android.

3. Guest mode and local storage

RunReel works without an account. Guest workouts, routes, weight records, health profile, goals, story content, and media references are stored locally in Android Room or Preferences DataStore. Exported images and videos are stored in user-visible device media. Local data remains until the user deletes it, clears app data, or uninstalls the app, subject to Android backup settings.

4. Optional account and Cloud Sync

Firebase Authentication is used for email/password and Google sign-in. Cloud Sync is off until a signed-in, verified user explicitly enables it. When enabled, RunReel may upload activity records, compressed GPS routes, laps, height, weight, goals, Route Story text, and optimized derivatives of selected Story images to Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage for backup, migration, synchronization, and device restore. Cloud data is isolated under the signed-in Firebase user ID. Turning sync off stops future sync but does not automatically erase existing cloud data.

5. Location

Precise location is requested when the user starts an outdoor RUN, WALK, HIKE, TRAIL_RUN, or CYCLE activity. TREADMILL does not require location. Location is used for route, distance, pace/speed, elevation, maps, summaries, stories, shares, and videos. Mapbox map requests may process IP address and device/request metadata. Users should avoid sharing route exports when revealing a home, workplace, or other sensitive location would create risk.

6. Photos and videos

RunReel uses Android Photo Picker and accesses only items the user selects. Selected media is used for Route Story, Share Studio, and Video Studio. Story images are uploaded only when Cloud Sync is enabled. Share/export actions send media only to a destination selected by the user. Generated exports remain until the user deletes them.

7. Service providers

  • Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Storage: account, optional synchronization, backup, and restore.
  • Firebase App Check: app/device attestation and abuse protection. Release builds use Play Integrity.
  • Mapbox: maps, terrain, route display, attribution, and map snapshots.
  • Google Play services Location: device location for outdoor activity recording.
  • Google Credential Manager: user-initiated Google sign-in.

RunReel 1.0 release builds are configured not to request or display advertising. Google Mobile Ads and UMP are used only in debug/testing builds unless a future release explicitly enables production advertising and updates this policy and Play disclosures first.

8. Purposes and legal choices

Information is processed to provide activity tracking, recovery, maps, history, goals, account security, optional synchronization, Route Story, shares, and videos; to prevent abuse; and to troubleshoot service failures. Users can remain a guest, deny optional permissions, avoid selecting media, keep Cloud Sync off, or stop using network-backed features.

9. Security and international processing

RunReel uses Android sandboxing, scoped storage, runtime permissions, authenticated Firebase rules, Firebase App Check, and HTTPS/TLS service connections. No system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process data in countries other than the user’s country under their terms and privacy policies.

10. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until deleted by the user, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Cloud-synced records remain until the user deletes the RunReel account/cloud data or the service removes them under an applicable legal requirement. The in-app path is Settings → Account → Delete Account. It deletes data only in the RunReel Firestore/Storage namespace, then requests deletion of the Firebase Authentication account. Because Firebase Authentication is shared across apps in the same Firebase project, deleting that authentication identity may also prevent sign-in to those other apps; RunReel warns before deletion. Other apps’ Firestore and Storage data is not deleted. An external request pathway will be published at the RunReel account-deletion page. No special retention is currently claimed after a completed deletion request, except where law, security, fraud prevention, or an unresolved request requires limited retention.

11. Children

RunReel is not intentionally directed to children. The final target-audience selection and availability must remain consistent with the app’s fitness, location, account, media, and any future advertising configuration.

RunReel 개인정보처리방침 요약
