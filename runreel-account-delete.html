Delete your account in the app

  1. Open RunReel.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion warning.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

If RunReel requires recent authentication, sign in again and repeat the deletion request shortly afterward.

Important: Deleting your RunReel account is intended to permanently remove your RunReel cloud data and the associated Firebase Authentication account. This action may not be reversible.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access RunReel, you can request deletion by email.

Send an email to: yeodolnim@gmail.com

Suggested subject: RunReel account deletion request

For account verification, please send the request from the email address used to create or sign in to your RunReel account.

Do not send your password, authentication token, Firebase UID, GPS coordinates, health information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Team DM Studio may request a limited verification step to confirm that the requester controls the relevant account.

Data deleted

When account deletion is completed, RunReel is designed to delete data associated with the RunReel account, including:

  • RunReel Firebase Authentication account;
  • RunReel Firestore profile and account-related records;
  • activity records, laps, cadence information, and related activity metadata;
  • RunReel Route Story titles, descriptions, and synchronized Story information;
  • weight history, height, target weight, and configured activity goals;
  • RunReel Cloud Storage GPS route backups;
  • synchronized Story image data or derivatives stored by RunReel.

Shared Firebase Authentication

RunReel uses a Firebase project that may also support other applications from the same developer.

RunReel account deletion is restricted to RunReel's own Firestore and Cloud Storage namespaces. RunReel does not intentionally delete another application's Firestore or Cloud Storage data.

However, the Firebase Authentication identity may be shared across applications in the same Firebase project. Deleting that authentication identity may therefore prevent the user from signing in to another application that uses the same Firebase Authentication account.

RunReel displays a warning before requesting deletion of the authentication identity.

Data not automatically deleted from your device

Account deletion does not necessarily remove every file stored locally on the user's device.

Local guest data, locally stored app data, downloaded or exported images, exported videos, and media already shared to another application may need to be deleted separately by the user.

Local data can be removed by using available delete functions, clearing RunReel app data, deleting exported media, or uninstalling the application, depending on the type of data.

Content already shared with another application or service is subject to that recipient's own storage and deletion policies.

Retention

RunReel does not currently claim a special retention period after a completed account-deletion request.

Limited information may be retained when required by law, or when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or completion of an unresolved deletion request.

RunReel 계정 삭제 안내

RunReel 앱에서 설정 → 계정 → 계정 삭제 메뉴를 통해 계정 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다.

앱을 사용할 수 없는 경우 RunReel에서 사용한 이메일 주소로 아래 지원 이메일에 계정 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다.

yeodolnim@gmail.com

이메일 제목은 RunReel account deletion request 로 작성할 수 있습니다.

비밀번호, 인증 토큰, Firebase UID, GPS 좌표, 건강정보 등 불필요한 민감정보는 이메일로 보내지 마세요.

계정 삭제가 완료되면 RunReel의 클라우드 운동 기록, 경로 백업, 랩 기록, Route Story 정보, 키·체중·목표체중·운동 목표 등의 데이터와 Firebase 인증 계정 삭제가 처리됩니다.

다만 Firebase Authentication 계정이 같은 개발자의 다른 앱과 공유되는 경우 해당 인증 계정을 삭제하면 다른 앱에서도 로그인할 수 없게 될 수 있습니다.

기기에 저장된 게스트 데이터, 내보낸 사진이나 동영상, 이미 다른 앱이나 서비스에 공유된 파일은 별도로 삭제해야 할 수 있습니다.
